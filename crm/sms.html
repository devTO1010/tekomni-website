TekOmniCRM Text Messages

Last updated: November 2026

TekOmniCRM, made and operated by TekOmni LLC, is client-management software used by small service offices, such as tax preparation offices. Through TekOmniCRM an office texts its own clients about their own service. TekOmniCRM sends every message and records every opt-in.

What you will receive

  • Appointment confirmations, reminders and changes
  • Requests for documents the office needs for your return or service
  • Status updates, for example that your return is ready to sign or was accepted
  • Replies from the office's staff to your questions about your service

No marketing or promotions. Every message starts with "TekOmniCRM:" and the office's name. We never ask for or send Social Security numbers or other tax ID numbers by text.

How to opt in

Text START to your office's TekOmniCRM number. The office shows this number at its front desk and on its intake forms. You will get one confirmation text. Offices cannot sign you up; only you can, from your own phone. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

The sign at the office reads:

Get texts from [Office name] about your appointments, documents and return status. Text START to [office number]. Sent by TekOmniCRM. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Privacy: tekomni.com/privacy.html · Terms: tekomni.com/terms.html

Frequency, cost, help and opt-out

  • Message frequency varies with your service, typically 2 to 8 messages per month during tax season.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to any message to stop all texts from that number. Reply START to rejoin.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact the office that texted you or TellMeMore@TekOmni.com.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Your privacy

We do not sell, rent or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing. See our Privacy Policy (section 8) and the Terms of Service (section 10, TekOmniCRM SMS Program).
